# Conservative normal modes

The `lagrangian_orbital` calculator accepts `subkind: lagrangian_modes` with square finite symmetric mass and stiffness matrices `M` and `K`. A verified real natural frequency requires a positive-definite mass matrix and nonnegative stiffness energy for every displacement. Each verified `eigenvalue_intervals_omega2` entry bounds the corresponding ordered eigenvalue of the supplied binary64 matrix pencil. The bounds concern arithmetic on the supplied matrices. Measurement, assembly and modeling uncertainty require separate inputs.

A zero interval denotes an exact rigid direction. A negative energy direction produces `unstable_negative_stiffness_eigenvalue`, including when a large mass gives a very small negative squared frequency. For example, `M=[[1e12]], K=[[-1]]` has squared frequency −10⁻¹² s⁻². A numerically unresolved positive frequency or unrepresentable squared frequency produces an unverified result. In that case the calculator withholds the frequency list.

Two numerical solvers find the frequencies. Independent power-of-four mass and stiffness scaling preserves their physical units. Exact dyadic congruence with the computed basis gives diagonal bounds on the mass and stiffness forms. Exact inertia of the stiffness form resolves uncertain signs and counts its null directions. For a positive mode whose enclosure is too wide, exact negative-direction counts for the pencil at both sides of the numerical estimate certify its relative interval of 10⁻¹⁰. The published end points are rounded outward. Verification also checks the eigenvectors, mass orthogonality and the dual positive frequencies.
