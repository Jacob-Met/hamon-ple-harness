# Axisymmetric pressure stress in a cylindrical annulus

The registered `solid_mohr_vessel_buckling` route with `subkind="pressure_vessel"` evaluates the Lamé solution at a material radius under signed internal and external pressure. Positive pressure means compressive radial traction. The result describes an axisymmetric, homogeneous wall in the long-cylinder region away from end disturbances.

Supply the inner radius `r_i` and either the outer radius `r_o` or positive wall thickness `t`. All three may be given if a common annulus satisfies `r_o = r_i + t`. Integer dimensions are exact. Numeric float dimensions used together describe their respective round-to-nearest binary64 cells. Two defining dimensions use their centers. With all three, admissible centers have priority; when the intersection requires an off-center representative the solver returns its dimensions. The sample radius `r_eval` defaults to the inner wall and must lie in the material at its supplied precision.

Let A = (pᵢ rᵢ² − pₒ rₒ²)/(rₒ² − rᵢ²) and D = (pᵢ − pₒ)rᵢ²rₒ²/[r²(rₒ² − rᵢ²)]. The radial and hoop stresses are A−D and A+D. Closed ends carry axial stress A, determined from their pressure resultant over the annular area. Freely open ends carry zero axial stress. The reported equivalent stress is √[3D²+(σz−A)²]. All stress outputs share the pressure input unit. The existing thin-wall fields retain their membrane conventions. [The Open University, thick-walled cylinder laboratory](https://oro.open.ac.uk/83722/1/Helen%20Lockett%20-%20Horizons%20in%20STEM%201st%20July%202020.pdf)

The `stress_split` object reports A, the signed amplitude D, the mean normal stress, and each principal deviatoric component before the individual total stresses are rounded for display. This retains a small nonzero shear on a closed cylinder whose three displayed stresses round to the same pressure. Verification checks both radial boundary tractions, the diametral hoop force, the end load and the invariant computed from all three stress differences. Public scalars must retain their sign and relative reporting accuracy; `residual` bounds relative publication errors, comparing equivalent stress in squared units.
