# Air-standard Otto and Diesel heat-addition states

Use the registered `thermo_cycles` mode with subkind `otto` or `diesel`. Supply compression ratio `r>1`. The inlet temperature `T1` (K), inlet absolute pressure `P1` (Pa), specific heat `cv` (J/kg K) and heat-capacity ratio `gamma>1` determine compression. Each value is positive and heat capacities are constant. Compression is isentropic. Heat enters at fixed volume in the Otto cycle and fixed pressure in the Diesel cycle. Each cycle expands isentropically to its original volume and rejects heat at fixed volume.

Give at least one prescription: peak temperature `T3` (K), positive heat added `q_in` (J/kg), or Diesel cutoff ratio `r_c`. The latter equals V3/V2 and must lie strictly between one and the compression ratio. When several values are supplied, they must describe a common heat-addition state. Whole-number prescriptions are exact and floating observations use their binary64 rounding cells, including ties to even. A single input selects its center. Supplying additional observations can resolve a small positive rise even when the displayed peak and compression temperatures coincide.

For r=4, T1=300 K, gamma=1.5 and cv=750 J/(kg K), compression reaches 600 K. Heating to 900 K requires 225,000 J/kg at fixed volume, or 337,500 J/kg at fixed pressure with a Diesel cutoff ratio of 1.5. The calculation returns the resolved temperature rise alongside the peak and exhaust temperatures, heat input, heat rejected, net work, thermal efficiency and mean effective pressure. It replays the heat balance against the independent integral of pressure over the compression, heating and expansion strokes.
