# Free-overflow spillway rating

The registered `spillway_weir` route uses the supplied dimensional SI coefficient `Cd_si`, effective crest length `weir_length_m`, and forward discharge `Q_design_m3_s`. It models an unsubmerged crest and treats approach velocity head as negligible so that upstream energy elevation equals reservoir water-surface elevation. For a positive flow, its unique head above crest is determined by `Q² = (Cd_si × weir_length_m)² H³`. The coefficient and crest length must be positive. Minimum freeboard and design discharge must be nonnegative. All elevations share one datum. [USACE HEC-RAS Hydraulic Reference Manual, overflow gates](https://www.hec.usace.army.mil/confluence/rasdocs/ras1dtechref/6.0/modeling-gated-spillways-weirs-and-drop-structures/hydraulic-computations-through-gated-spillways/overflow-gates)

With positive discharge, the route returns the required effective head, upstream stage, signed freeboard above that stage, allowable discharge at the minimum freeboard, and the inclusive freeboard decision. It decides feasibility using the available head measured from crest to the freeboard limit. For example, 1 m³/s over a crest of unit coefficient and unit length requires exactly 1 m of head. If dam top equals crest, freeboard is −1 m under every elevation datum.

At zero discharge, every stage at or below crest is compatible. Without an observed stage the effective overflow head is zero, `water_surface_el_m` and `freeboard_m` are null, and `water_surface_el_upper_bound_m` and `freeboard_lower_bound_m` give the attainable endpoint. `passes_freeboard` is true when every such stage meets the requirement and otherwise null. The latter means some possible stages pass and some fail. Supplying `water_surface_el_m` fixes the no-overflow stage and permits a definite answer. For positive discharge a supplied integer stage is an exact constraint and a supplied binary64 stage is an observation rounded to that precision.

The calculation uses subtraction of elevations at their admitted precision before reporting any individual absolute elevation. The reported absolute stage can coincide with the rounded crest even when the relative head is nonzero. Inputs that would produce an unreportable head, signed clearance, capacity, or stage decline verification.
